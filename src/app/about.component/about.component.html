Our Journey

Built on Experience,
Driven by Innovation

Founded in 2019 in Bengaluru, Blute Technologies was built on a simple belief — that enterprises deserve technology partners who are as invested in their success as they are. From a small founding team with deep enterprise software roots, we've grown into a trusted digital transformation partner serving clients across industries and continents.

Today, with 43+ full-time professionals, two offices, and partnerships spanning the USA, Singapore, Africa and India, we help organizations untangle complex challenges and stay permanently ahead of the technology curve.

2019 — Founded in Bengaluru

Launched with a founding team of enterprise technology veterans with a mission to democratize cutting-edge solutions.

2021 — Scaled to 20+ Clients

Expanded capabilities across cloud, IoT and mobile — delivering large-scale solutions to global enterprises.

2023 — Global Presence Established

Opened strategic partnerships in USA, Singapore and Africa — expanding our delivery and consulting footprint worldwide.

2024–25 — Agentic AI Leadership

Pioneering autonomous AI agent solutions, helping enterprises automate complex workflows and unlock the power of LLMs.
