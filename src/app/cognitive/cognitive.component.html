OVERVIEW

What is Cognitive Computing?

Cognitive computing refers to technology platforms that, broadly speaking, are based on the scientific disciplines of artificial intelligence and signal processing. These platforms encompass machine learning, reasoning, natural language processing, speech recognition and vision, human–computer interaction, dialog and narrative generation and more.

At Blute Technologies, we build cognitive systems that continuously learn and adapt to provide contextual, personalized experiences. Our solutions enable computers to comprehend complex data, recognize patterns, and generate insights that augment human decision-making.
